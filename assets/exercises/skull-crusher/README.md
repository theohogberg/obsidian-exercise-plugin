# Skull Crusher

![Start position](0.jpg) ![End position](1.jpg)

## Cues

Upper arms vertical and fixed.

## Setup

Lie on a bench holding an EZ-bar over your chest with straight arms, tilted slightly back towards your head.

## Steps

1. Bend only at the elbows to lower the bar towards your forehead.
2. Straighten your arms back to the start without moving your upper arms.
